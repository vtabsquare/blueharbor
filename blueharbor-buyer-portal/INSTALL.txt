BUYER LOGIN PATCH

1. Stop the buyer app with Ctrl+C in its terminal.
2. Back up your existing app/page.tsx and backend/supabase_auth.py.
3. Copy the app and backend folders from this ZIP into:
   D:\Python\Project Blueharbor\blueharbor-buyer-portal
4. Accept replacement of those two files only. Do not delete either folder.
5. Run run_local.py again and refresh the browser.

This corrects the local length restriction, not every possible Supabase rejection.
